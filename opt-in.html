SMS Opt-In Process

Michael Egan personal: automated alerts and reminders

Step 1: verbal request

Consent is collected verbally, in person or by phone, by Michael Egan. He reads this script:

"I can send you automated text alerts and reminders from my personal scripts, like appointment reminders and notices when a task finishes or fails. It's usually a few texts a month. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. At any time you can reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. The terms are at eganator.github.io/sms-policy/terms.html and the privacy policy is at eganator.github.io/sms-policy. Would you like to receive these texts, yes or no?"

If the person says yes, Michael replies: "Great, you'll get a text shortly asking you to confirm your enrollment."

Step 2: confirmation by text

The number then receives this confirmation, and no other messages are sent until the person replies YES:

Michael Egan personal: You asked to receive automated alerts and reminders from Michael Egan. Reply YES to confirm. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

The YES reply is kept in the message log as the record of consent.

Step 3: enrollment

Michael Egan personal: You're subscribed to automated alerts and reminders. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

See also the SMS Terms and Conditions and SMS Privacy Policy.
