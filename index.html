Michael Egan sends occasional automated text messages to family members and personal contacts who have asked to receive them, such as appointment reminders, caregiving and household updates, and alerts from personal scripts.

Your mobile number and opt-in consent are never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or any other purpose. The number is used only to send you these messages, through the Twilio messaging service.

Varies, typically a few messages per month.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opting out and help
